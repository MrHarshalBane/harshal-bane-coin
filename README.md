## 🛠️ How to Deploy a Solana Token on Devnet (No-Code Guide)

Yes, you can easily deploy a Solana token on the devnet for free without writing a single line of code. Several reputable "no-code" platforms let you fill out a simple web form to deploy standard SPL tokens with images and metadata.

### Step 1: Prep Your Wallet
Before navigating to a tool, you need a web wallet like Phantom switched to Devnet mode and funded with free test SOL.
1. **Switch to Devnet**: Open your Phantom wallet, go to **Settings > Developer Settings**, and toggle on **Testnet Mode** (choose Devnet).
2. **Get Free Test SOL**: Visit an official faucet such as the Solana Faucet or use the **"Airdrop"** button directly inside your wallet settings. Request 1 or 2 SOL.
   - [1] [Phantom Devnet Video Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a7PCWniWzg8)
   - [2] [OnFinality Solana Devnet Guide](https://onfinality.io/en/rpc-assistant/solana-devnet-guide)
   - [3] [Deploying Token Smart Contract on Solana (Medium)](https://medium.com/@0xsupremedev/deploying-your-first-token-smart-contract-on-the-solana-blockchain-df275282a405)

### Step 2: Choose a No-Code Devnet Deployer
Use one of these verified, visual tool alternatives that feature dedicated devnet toggles:
- **20lab Solana Token Creator**: A fully featured tool that operates flawlessly on the devnet network.
- **CreateMyCoin Solana Token Deployer**: Allows completely free test runs to launch your token architecture directly into your wallet.
- **DeployAToken**: A multi-chain tool allowing simple parameter configurations and automated token authority revocations on test networks.
  - [1] [20lab SPL Token Generator](https://20lab.app/generate/spl-token/)
  - [2] [CreateMyCoin App](https://createmycoin.app/)
  - [3] [CreateMyCoin Solana Deployer](https://createmycoin.app/solana-token-deployer)
  - [4] [DeployAToken Platform](https://deployatoken.com/)

### Step 3: Fill Out the Token Parameters
Once your wallet is connected to your platform of choice, fill in your asset information:

| Field | What to Enter |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Token Name** | The full name of your cryptocurrency (e.g., `Devnet Sandbox Token`). |
| **Token Symbol / Ticker** | The 3–5 letter shorthand (e.g., `SANDBOX`). |
| **Decimals** | Standard Solana fungible tokens default to `6` or `9`. (`6` is recommended if you plan to practice liquidity pool generation). |
| **Total Supply** | How many coins you want to mint immediately into your wallet. |
| **Token Logo / Image** | Upload a square JPEG/PNG picture. The platform automatically anchors this to the decentralized metadata profile. |

### Step 4: Deploy and Confirm
1. Click **Create Token** or **Deploy**.
2. Your wallet extension will prompt you to **sign a transaction**.
3. Review the transaction parameters (it should only cost a fraction of your free devnet SOL for gas) and click **Confirm**.
   - [1] [Deploy & Sign Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9R7mRaonVoo)
   - [2] [SolTokenCreator Devnet Blog](https://www.soltokencreator.io/blog/create-solana-token-devnet)
   - [3] [20lab Generator](https://20lab.app/generate/spl-token/)

Within **5 to 10 seconds**, the transaction will finalize. You can copy the generated **Mint Address** and paste it into the [Solana Explorer](https://explorer.solana.com/?cluster=devnet) (remember to change the explorer network dropdown to Devnet) to view your token globally.
- [1] [Mint Address Walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YxH525719Zw&t=221)
- [2] [Solana Explorer Verification](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JJe9oh15xRI&t=252)

### 💡 Pro Testing Tips
- **Importing Token into Phantom**: Under Devnet mode, click "Manage Token List", search for your newly minted Mint Address, and toggle it on to view it in your wallet.
- **Revoking Authorities (Locking Supply)**: Practice revoking Mint Authority (to lock total supply), Freeze Authority (so tokens cannot be frozen), and Update Authority (to make metadata immutable).
